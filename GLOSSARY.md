# Gensai

Domain vocabulary for disaster preparedness in Japan.

## Language

**Gensai map**:
The map experience for examining mapped hazards around a selected location in Japan.
_Avoid_: Gensai entry point, when referring to the map itself.

**Gensai entry point**:
Gensai's main site address, distinct from the address of the Gensai map.
_Avoid_: Gensai map, when referring to the main site address.

**Current resident**:
A person who already lives in Japan.
_Avoid_: User, when referring specifically to residency status.

**Home**:
The place where a current resident lives.
_Avoid_: Current location, which may be somewhere other than the resident's home.

**Selected location**:
The geographic point a person chooses to examine for mapped hazards. It may be
their home, workplace, or another place.
_Avoid_: Home, when referring to a point that may represent another place.

**Current location**:
The device's estimated geographic position. It may differ from the selected
location.
_Avoid_: Selected location, unless the person has chosen their device's position.

**Hazard layer**:
A map representation of a particular hazard type or scenario.
_Avoid_: Live conditions, which implies information about what is happening now.

**Coverage**:
The geographic extent for which a particular hazard dataset provides information.
Coverage does not establish that an uncoloured place is safe.
_Avoid_: Hazard area, which describes where a hazard is mapped rather than where
information is available.
